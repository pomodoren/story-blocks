A story that is also a talk

Press Present and this page becomes a slide deck. Every block is a slide; each section is a column. All figures are illustrative. Try →, ↓, O, F, B, ?.

Section 1 · Navigate

A cover or a level-1/2 heading opens a section. Press → to jump to the next one.

Text

Two kinds of movement

← → move between sections. ↑ ↓ and Space walk every slide in order. Home and End jump to the ends.

Deep links

The address bar tracks #/N while presenting, so any slide can be linked. Opening ?present starts the deck straight away.
